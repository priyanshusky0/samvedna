    Mann-Kendall trend test asks whether a weekly series rises or falls consistently, without assuming a straight line or normal data. Sen's slope is the median rate of change. Weather (5 years) uses the Seasonal test, which compares each week only with the same week in other years, so the annual cycle can't pose as a trend (slope per year). Shorter series use the Hamed-Rao test on the last 52 weeks (slope per week); that describes direction within the window and may reflect the season. p < 0.05 = significant.
    Lagged Spearman correlation between a daily hazard signal and next-day news volume (lag k: news k days after the hazard). A peak at k > 0 means coverage follows events.
